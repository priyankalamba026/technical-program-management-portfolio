How the model works

Available = engineers × (weekdays − holidays) − total team time off

Allocated = available × allocation %

Planned capacity = allocated × (1 − buffer %)

Headroom or shortfall = planned capacity − estimated demand

What this helps decide

When demand exceeds capacity, adjust scope, timing, or allocation and compare the trade-offs. Increasing allocation also reduces time available for other work.

Assumptions & limits
  • Engineer-days represent effort, not elapsed calendar time or a guarantee of delivery.
  • This example treats engineers as interchangeable and assumes a shared holiday calendar.
  • Skills, dependencies, partial-period staffing, and differences in individual availability require a more detailed model.
  • Demand estimates and the planning buffer are assumptions that should be reviewed with the team.
