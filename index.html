Taking an AI Returns Workflow into Production

Led cross-functional program execution for a customer-service AI returns workflow, aligning evaluation, platform integrations, privacy, and launch readiness through a phased rollout.

AI evaluationPrivacy & latencyPlatform dependenciesPhased rollout
Explore the case study
Challenge
Bring an AI workflow into production while quality, privacy requirements, and shared-platform dependencies were still evolving.
My role
Led the integrated program plan, coordinated engineering, product, operations, and security partners, and made dependencies, risks, and readiness visible for launch decisions.
Decisions & trade-offs
Coordinated resolution of privacy-redaction and latency issues, tracked evaluation coverage and gaps, and aligned staged expansion with validation, monitoring, and fallback readiness.
What I changed
Connected capability plans, dependency tracking, risk reviews, and readiness gates so teams could assess launch confidence beyond task completion.
Outcome
The AI self-service returns workflow reached 50% of U.S. traffic through a phased rollout. This is a program-level result achieved by the cross-functional team.
